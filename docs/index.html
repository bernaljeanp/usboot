Bootable USB drives,
every byte verified.

USBoot writes any ISO to a USB stick, then reads it all back to prove it. A built-in library always fetches the newest Ubuntu, Fedora, Mint or Arch from the fastest mirrors. A 3 MB app, free and open source.

Windows 10/11 · Linux & macOS (beta) · all downloads

Everything Rufus and Etcher do. Then some.

Built in Rust, so it's tiny and fast. Nothing to configure: pick an image, pick a drive.

Verified, byte for byte

After writing, USBoot reads the whole drive back and compares it. A faulty or fake stick can't hide.

Live ISO library

20 distros, always the newest official release, read straight from each project's checksum file. No stale links.

Multi-mirror downloads

Measures the official mirrors, drops the slow ones and pulls from several at once. One test went from 44 KB/s to 16 MB/s.

Windows 11, your way

Install on PCs without TPM 2.0 or Secure Boot, with a local account instead of a Microsoft one, and no privacy questions.

Several drives at once

The same image onto every stick in parallel, each one verified on its own. Made for classrooms and repair shops.

Restore any drive

One click back to a normal stick with its full capacity. Fixes the "my 32 GB drive shows 4 MB" after flashing Linux.

The library

Every download is checked against the project's official SHA-256 before you can use it.
